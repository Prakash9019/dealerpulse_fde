# Lead Escalation & Delivery Recovery SOP

## Purpose

This procedure governs when and how a branch manager or sales officer
escalates a stalled lead or an at-risk delivery, so that revenue already won
(an order placed) is never lost to inaction after the sale is effectively
closed.

## When to escalate

A lead must be escalated to the branch manager within one business day when
any of the following is true:

1. The lead has reached **Order Placed** and has had no recorded activity for
   **8 or more days** (DealerPulse's stale threshold). At this stage the
   revenue is already committed — the risk is purely fulfilment, not sales.
2. A lead's expected close date has passed (`overdue`) and it is still open.
3. A lead is flagged **Critical** or **High-value stale** in DealerPulse's
   Action Center, and the assigned rep has not logged a contact attempt
   within 48 hours of the flag appearing.

## Escalation steps

1. **Assign an explicit owner.** Every escalated lead must have one named
   accountable person — either the original rep (if they are actively
   working it) or the branch manager (if the rep has been unresponsive).
2. **Confirm delivery logistics.** For Order Placed leads, confirm vehicle
   allocation status and a firm delivery date with the customer within 24
   hours of escalation.
3. **Log the touch.** Every escalation must be logged with a timestamp and a
   note describing the action taken — this closes the loop DealerPulse's
   lead-aging tracker is measuring against.
4. **Branch manager review.** If a lead remains unresolved 3 business days
   after escalation, the branch manager must review it personally and decide
   between: re-engaging the customer directly, reassigning the lead to
   another rep, or marking it lost with a documented reason.

## Delay-reason handling

When a delivery is delayed, the delay reason must be one of the recognised
categories (vehicle allocation, financing approval, customer request,
documentation, logistics) so that DealerPulse's delivery-operations analytics
can attribute the delay correctly. An undocumented delay is treated the same
as "no reason recorded" and will not be excluded from the delay-rate metric.

## Non-goals

This SOP does not cover discount approval thresholds or financing
underwriting — those are handled by the separate Deal Desk policy.
